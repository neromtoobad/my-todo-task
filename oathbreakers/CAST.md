# OATHBREAKERS - Cast (v1, locked)

Direction: global mix, invented celebrities. Every character is fictional and is not based on any real person.

Trait sliders (1-5) drive the game engine:
- **Perception** - how well they read evidence and people
- **Deception** - how convincingly they lie as an Oathbreaker
- **Loudness** - how often they speak up and how much they sway the table
- **Loyalty** - how hard they stick with allies (and how hard they take betrayal)

Tells only appear some of the time, and mostly when the character is lying. They show up as stage directions in dialogue (for example *closes her notebook*) or in mission results, so an attentive player can learn to read them.

## 1. Adaeze "Ada" Okonkwo - 36 - Lagos, Nigeria
- **Famous for:** six seasons as the lead in the Nollywood crime hit *Inspector Ada*. She played a detective for so long that she now thinks she is one.
- **At the table:** keeps a leather notebook, opens every Round Table with "I've been investigating..."
- **Traits:** Perception 3, Deception 3, Loudness 5, Loyalty 3
- **As Sworn:** leads accusations with dramatic theories. Right about half the time, and she swings a lot of votes either way.
- **As Oathbreaker:** "investigates" loudly and builds a case against a scapegoat.
- **Tell:** *closes her notebook* when she is lying.
- **Voice:** Nigerian English, rich and theatrical, loves a dramatic pause.
- **Look:** emerald silk headwrap, gold hoops, tailored camel trench coat, leather notebook in hand.
- **Signature line:** "Case closed. It's you."

## 2. Rafael "Rafa" Duarte - 28 - Sao Paulo, Brazil
- **Famous for:** superstar striker, famous for wild goal celebrations and red cards.
- **At the table:** all heart, no filter. Picks a side fast and defends it to the end.
- **Traits:** Perception 2, Deception 2, Loudness 5, Loyalty 5
- **As Sworn:** fiercely protects his friends and explodes when betrayed.
- **As Oathbreaker:** overcompensates with loyalty speeches.
- **Tell:** swears on his mother ("on my mother's life") far more often when lying.
- **Voice:** Brazilian accent, fast, passionate, mixes in Portuguese.
- **Look:** gold chain, fitted black knit polo, tattoo sleeve, messy curls, bright grin.
- **Signature line:** "I would never. On my mother!"
- **Link:** Zara's ex. Their breakup was tabloid front pages for a month.

## 3. Han Min-seo - 24 - Seoul, South Korea
- **Famous for:** the center of a global K-pop girl group. Media-trained since she was 15.
- **At the table:** polite, smiling, underestimated. Quietly the sharpest observer in the castle.
- **Traits:** Perception 5, Deception 4, Loudness 2, Loyalty 4
- **As Sworn:** says little and drops one devastating observation per Round Table.
- **As Oathbreaker:** the most dangerous one. Nobody suspects the polite one.
- **Tell:** her answers get shorter and extra polite when she is lying.
- **Voice:** soft, careful English, and she switches to Korean when shocked.
- **Look:** sleek dark bob, cream oversized blazer, pearl earrings, stage-ready makeup.
- **Signature line:** "I noticed something. Maybe it's nothing."
- **Link:** bonds fast with Tayla ("the kids' table").

## 4. Dame Celia Hartwell - 68 - London, UK

- **At the table:** commanding, theatrical, treats the Round Table as her stage.

- **Link:** Marcus once roasted her on his show, and she has never forgiven him.
